## Technical Details

- **Total Glyphs**: 3,816
- **Extended-A (U+13460-U+143FF)**: 2,918 glyphs
- **Core Block (U+13000-U+1342F)**: 898 glyphs
- **Source**: JSesh SVG glyphs + Unikemet Unicode mapping
- **Generation**: FontForge
